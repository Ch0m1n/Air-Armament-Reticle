Methods & limitations

Bomb dimensions use the current MKtool estimate: W = round(60 × salvoX); H = round(60 × maxSpreadY / maxSpreadX × salvoY) + round(2.69 × speedMoveWithBomb × (attackCount − 1) × attackInterval). Green is the estimated minimum (fully aimed) outline; dashed amber is the maximum outline. Minimum width = maximum width × minSpreadX / maxSpreadX; minimum height = (maximum height − movement offset) × minSpreadY / maxSpreadY + movement offset. Dashed red is the innerSalvoSize region scaled to full aim with the same minSpread / maxSpread ratios. Its percentage is a bomb allocation parameter, not target hit probability; the actual RNG remains unverified. Absolute dimensions, coefficients, dynamic aiming and build effects are not independently verified.

Torpedo sectors interpolate from formations.attack[1] X span to minSpread at torpedoAimDist, then extend to the visible distance. Coordinates use 30 m per raw unit. Both axes and the 263 m Yamato reference share one metric scale. No near-parallel angle threshold or fixed fan multiplier is used. Current-client boundary padding, ship render magnification, partial attack groups, individual payload spacing and actual trajectories are not reproduced. Manual direction selections are illustrations only.

Load the referenced torpedo Projectile JSON for range and arming data. Range = maxDist × 30 m; arming distance = speed × armingTime × 2.686 m, matching MKtool. Each sector extends to its own maximum torpedo range. Moving the target does not change the viewing scale. Blank means unarmed; green means armed.

Old aircraft files can remain in GameParams. Confirm the aircraft reference in the correct ship's torpedo-bomber component. Formation mismatches are not guessed away.
